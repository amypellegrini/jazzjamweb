Building the minor 7th block chords

In Part 1 we harmonised a single chord, C6. The melodies we play move over different kinds of chords, though, so harmonising a major chord isn't enough on its own: to play a melody in block chords, you need to know how to harmonise each kind of chord it moves over.

The goal of the parts that follow is to play a 2 5 1 progression in block chords. In C major, that is Dm7, G7 and C6. We already know the 1 chord, so we start with the 2: the minor 7th chord, Dm7.

The block is built exactly as in the first lesson, now with the notes of Dm7: D, F, A and C.

Exercise Preparation for block chords scale in Dm7

Play all positions of the Dm7 block chord in ascending and descending order, one block per beat: the melody on D, F, A and C, up to the D an octave higher and back down again. The melody always doubled an octave lower in the left hand.
