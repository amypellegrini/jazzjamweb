2 5 1 passing tones

Harmonise passing tones with the chords of a major 2 5 1.

In C major, the 2 5 1 chords are Dm7, G7 and C6. The chord tones C, E and G stay on C6, the 1 chord. The passing tones D, F and B are harmonised as G7, the 5 chord. The sixth degree, A, is harmonised as Dm7, the 2 chord.

Voicing the sixth degree as Dm7 has a particular effect on the way up: the last three notes, A, B and C, are harmonised as Dm7, G7 and C6, a complete 2 5 1 progression that carries the melody home to the top of the scale.

If we use the chromatic passing tones technique to approach Dm7 we can introduce a new chord for the G♯/A♭ passing tone: C♯m7.

Coming down the scale, the G♯/A♭ chromatic passing tone keeps the same chord as in Example 16.

Exercise 2 5 1 passing tones, up and down

Play the C bebop scale up and back down in eighth notes, one block per note: C6 on C, E and G; G7 on D, F and B; G7♭9 on A♭; and Dm7 on A.

The goal is to hear every passing tone resolve back to C6, as a small 2 5 1, in both directions.
