Block chords, also called locked hands, give every note of a melody its own chord, so a single line at the piano sounds like a full horn section. It's the sound of George Shearing, Red Garland and Oscar Peterson.

In six short lessons you'll build the basic block shape, practise it on Barry Harris's sixth-diminished scale, and learn to harmonise the passing tones between chord tones: with diminished, diatonic and chromatic chords, and with the chords of a 2 5 1.

Every example comes with sheet music, audio and a keyboard chart that plays along, and every lesson ends with an exercise to practise at the piano.
